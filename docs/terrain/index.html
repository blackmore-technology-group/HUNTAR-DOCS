AR Terrain Intelligence

Read the terrain overlays as structured decision support, not as a guarantee of wildlife presence.

Terrain modes

ModeField purpose
BeddingTerrain relationships relevant to bedding-oriented habitat/use analysis.

Terrain-following presentation

HuntAR is designed to attach intelligence to terrain structure rather than display broad screen-space blobs when metric spatial evidence is available. Bounded terrain-following regions/corridors are presented separately from hard safety exclusions.

Score bands

70–100 · High
40–69 · Medium
0–39 · Low

These are relative analysis bands. They do not mean “70–100% chance an animal is standing here.” Habitat/use intelligence, detectability and actual presence are different concepts.

Terrain confidence

The HUD terrain-confidence instrument describes confidence in current terrain/spatial context. Low confidence is a reason to reduce reliance on precise overlay placement. Camera tracking, GNSS conditions, terrain data and device state can all affect performance.

Explainability

Where an overlay exposes detail, use it as context for why a region was surfaced. Combine that explanation with direct observation, current conditions and authoritative legal information.

Never treat a score or overlay as legal permission, safe passage, proof of animal presence or permission to take a shot.
