Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to common questions before or during field use.

Does HuntAR work without cell service?

Core offline workflows are designed around installed Hunt Areas, local maps and local-first waypoints. Connected features require the network/service they depend on.

Does a High score mean an animal is there?

No. High/Medium/Low are relative analysis bands, not a guarantee of animal presence.

Can I use HuntAR instead of checking regulations?

No. Always use current authoritative regulations and independently verify closures, land access and boundaries.

Are historic trails safe to follow?

Not necessarily. Historic/legacy lines may be abandoned, overgrown, relocated or impassable.

Are waypoints stored in the cloud?

Waypoints are local-first. Cloud synchronization is optional where a connected service is configured.

Why does HuntAR need precise location?

For offline navigation, Hunt Area context, routes, waypoints and spatial alignment. See the Privacy Policy.

Which species are selectable?

Current v4.3.0 UI profiles are Elk, Mule Deer, Whitetail Deer, Moose, Black Bear and Turkey. Waterfowl is listed but unavailable pending its dedicated waterway addition.

What if an AR overlay looks wrong?

Stop relying on precise placement. Check terrain confidence and GPS quality, let the system relocalize, and use the 2D map plus independent navigation until alignment is trustworthy again.
