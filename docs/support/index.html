Product support

Do not publish credentials, private ENTITY bindings, precise private-location history or other sensitive information in public channels.

Safety and field-use boundary

HuntAR is a field-assistance and navigation application. It does not guarantee animal presence, legal access, boundary accuracy, safe terrain, weather conditions, emergency response or regulatory compliance. Wildlife-probability overlays are decision-support information, not a guarantee.

  • Verify current hunting regulations with the responsible authority.
  • Verify private-land permission, closures, protected areas and no-hunt zones independently.
  • Carry appropriate offline maps, navigation equipment and emergency supplies.
  • Do not rely on HuntAR as your only method of navigation or emergency communication.
  • Use lawful and safe hunting practices and comply with local rules.
