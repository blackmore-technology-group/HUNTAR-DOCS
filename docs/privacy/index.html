HuntAR · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 3, 2026  ·  Application: HuntAR for iOS

HuntAR uses location, camera and motion capabilities to provide spatial and navigation functionality. HuntAR does not use third-party advertising trackers and the current iOS privacy manifest declares tracking disabled.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy explains how Blackmore Technology Group Limited (“BTG”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in connection with the HuntAR mobile application and related HuntAR services.

2. Information HuntAR processes

Depending on the features you use and permissions you grant, HuntAR may process the following categories of information:

  • Precise location. HuntAR uses device location while the app is in use for offline navigation, route context, terrain positioning, waypoints, spatial overlays and user-authorized safety functionality. The current iOS privacy manifest identifies precise location for app functionality, not tracking.
  • Camera and spatial sensor information. Camera imagery, ARKit information, motion and heading sensors are used to maintain spatial orientation and render terrain-aware augmented reality. These sensors are used to provide the feature being requested.
  • Device and application information. HuntAR may process device model, operating-system version, application version, timestamps, application settings, installation identifiers and device identifiers used for security, provenance, synchronization or qualification.
  • ENTITY identity and provenance information. When ENTITY integration is configured and an active principal binding is supplied, HuntAR may use a principal ENTITY identifier, installation ENTITY identifier, device ENTITY identifier, binding hashes, event identifiers and provenance metadata to associate authorized events with the correct user, installation and device.
  • User-created field information. Features may process waypoints, routes, observations, selections and other field actions you create. Some information can remain local to the device; information used with synchronization, sharing or configured services may be transmitted as required for that feature.
  • Technical and qualification information. HuntAR may process operational measurements such as location accuracy, render timing, thermal/battery state, application state and related evidence when performing device qualification, diagnostics or field-validation functions.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide HuntAR functionality; position AR content; support offline navigation and route context; maintain device and installation integrity; synchronize or share information when a user invokes supported connected features; establish provenance and authorized ENTITY events; protect the service; diagnose failures; and qualify device/software performance.

4. Tracking, advertising and sale of data

HuntAR does not use information for cross-company advertising tracking. The current iOS privacy manifest declares tracking disabled and no tracking domains. BTG does not sell personal information collected through HuntAR.

5. ENTITY integration

HuntAR includes an ENTITY client designed for governed identity, provenance and data-rights workflows. ENTITY integration is inactive unless an API endpoint and valid binding are configured. The native capture path is designed to record identifiers, hashes, event metadata, application context and rights/provenance information. Where the capture format marks raw_content_included as false, raw application content is not included in that ENTITY provenance envelope.

6. Permissions

HuntAR may request permission for camera access, motion/heading information and precise location while the app is in use. You can control these permissions through iOS Settings. Disabling a permission can prevent features that depend on it from functioning correctly.

7. Local storage and retention

HuntAR stores certain settings, bindings, offline information and queued records on the device. Local information can remain until removed by the application, cleared by the user where supported, or removed when the application is uninstalled. Information transmitted to a configured service may be retained as needed to provide the requested feature, preserve security or provenance records, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes or maintain service integrity. Retention depends on the type of record and the service involved.

8. Sharing and disclosures

HuntAR may transmit information to BTG-operated or user-configured services when required for synchronization, ENTITY functionality, shared-field features or other connected functionality. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a lawful corporate transaction. We do not authorize third parties to use HuntAR data for their own cross-app advertising tracking.

9. Security

HuntAR uses platform security features and encrypted network transport for configured non-local service endpoints. Device provenance features can use Apple security hardware where available. No system can guarantee absolute security; users should protect their device credentials and avoid entering sensitive information into public support channels.

10. Location and safety information

Location, mapping, wildlife-probability, safety and land-context information may be incomplete, delayed or inaccurate. HuntAR is an assistance tool. Users remain responsible for checking current hunting regulations, land permissions, closures, boundaries, weather, emergency information and local conditions through authoritative sources.

11. Children

HuntAR is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed as a child-focused service. Users must comply with applicable age and hunting-licence requirements in their jurisdiction.

12. Your choices

You may deny or revoke iOS permissions, stop using connected HuntAR services, remove the application from your device, and contact BTG regarding privacy questions. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, provenance, legal compliance or dispute resolution.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as HuntAR changes. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published. Material changes affecting App Store privacy disclosures will be reflected in the relevant application release and App Store Connect information.

14. Contact

HuntAR is developed by Blackmore Technology Group Limited, British Columbia, Canada. For general product or privacy contact pathways, use the HuntAR Support page or visit Blackmore Technology Group. Do not place private keys, passwords, precise private-location history, medical information or other sensitive personal information into a public GitHub issue.

App Store disclosure boundary: this policy describes the current HuntAR iOS architecture and is intended to be read together with the privacy disclosures submitted in App Store Connect. If functionality or data flows change, both this policy and those disclosures must be updated.
