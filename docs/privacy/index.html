Privacy Policy

How HuntAR handles information used for spatial, navigation, offline and connected functionality.

Effective: October 3, 2026 · Application: HuntAR for iOS

Summary: HuntAR uses location, camera and motion capabilities for spatial/navigation functionality. The current iOS privacy manifest declares tracking disabled and no tracking domains.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains how Blackmore Technology Group Limited (“BTG”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in connection with HuntAR and related HuntAR services.

2. Information HuntAR processes

  • Precise location: offline navigation, Hunt Area context, routes, waypoints and terrain positioning.
  • Camera and spatial sensors: ARKit, augmented reality, orientation and spatial alignment.
  • Device/application information: device model, OS/app version, timestamps, settings and identifiers used for security, provenance, synchronization or qualification.
  • ENTITY identity/provenance information: when configured, principal, installation and device ENTITY identifiers, binding hashes, event identifiers and provenance metadata.
  • User-created field information: waypoints, routes, observations, selections and other field actions.
  • Technical/qualification information: operational measurements such as location accuracy, render timing and device state where those functions are used.

3. How information is used

To provide spatial/navigation functionality, maintain integrity, support selected connected features, establish provenance for authorized ENTITY events, protect the service, diagnose failures and qualify software/device performance.

4. Tracking, advertising and sale

HuntAR does not use information for cross-company advertising tracking. The current iOS privacy manifest declares tracking disabled and no tracking domains. BTG does not sell personal information collected through HuntAR.

5. ENTITY integration

ENTITY integration is inactive unless an endpoint and valid binding are configured. The native capture path uses identifiers, hashes, event metadata, application context and rights/provenance information. Where an envelope marks raw_content_included false, raw application content is not included in that envelope.

6. Permissions

You can control camera, motion and location permissions through iOS Settings. Disabling a permission can prevent dependent features from working.

7. Local storage and retention

HuntAR stores certain settings, bindings, offline information and queued records on-device. Local data can remain until removed by the app/user where supported or when the application is uninstalled. Connected-service retention depends on the record and service.

8. Sharing and disclosures

Information may be sent to BTG-operated or user-configured services when required for synchronization, ENTITY functionality or selected connected features, and may be disclosed where required by law or to protect users/service integrity.

9. Security

Configured non-local ENTITY endpoints require HTTPS. HuntAR uses platform security capabilities and can use hardware-backed provenance functions where available. No system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Location and safety

Location, mapping, wildlife scoring, safety and land context can be incomplete, delayed or inaccurate. Users remain responsible for checking authoritative regulations, permissions, closures, weather and local conditions.

11. Children

HuntAR is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed as a child-focused service.

12. Your choices

You may revoke permissions, stop using connected services and remove the application. Some records may need retention for security, provenance, compliance or dispute resolution.

13. Changes

We may update this policy as HuntAR changes. Material changes affecting App Store privacy disclosures will be reflected in the relevant release.

14. Contact

HuntAR is developed by Blackmore Technology Group Limited, British Columbia, Canada. Use the HuntAR Support page or BTG website.
