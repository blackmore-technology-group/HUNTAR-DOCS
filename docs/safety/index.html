Safety & Legal Boundaries

HuntAR can help you reason about the field. It cannot make a hunt legal, a route safe or a prediction certain.

What HuntAR is

HuntAR is a field-assistance and navigation tool. It can present terrain intelligence, map context, routes, waypoints, wind/visibility information and safety-aware spatial layers.

What HuntAR is not

  • Not an authoritative source of hunting regulations.
  • Not proof of land ownership, permission or legal access.
  • Not a replacement for emergency services or dedicated emergency communication.
  • Not a guarantee of trail condition, road access, wildfire safety or weather.
  • Not proof that wildlife is present in a scored region.
  • Not a substitute for safe firearm handling, target identification or backstop awareness.

Legal and exclusion layers

HuntAR treats safety/legal exclusions separately from wildlife-scoring layers. A high terrain score does not override a no-hunt zone, closure, private-property restriction or other exclusion.

Field verification checklist

  1. Check current regulations for species, season, weapon and management unit.
  2. Verify land ownership/access and local closures.
  3. Check wildfire, weather and road/trail conditions.
  4. Confirm location with more than one method when consequences are significant.
  5. Identify the target and what is beyond it independently of the app.
  6. Keep a backup navigation and emergency plan.
Emergency use: do not rely on HuntAR as your sole emergency communication or rescue system. Use appropriate emergency/SAR channels and dedicated communication equipment.
