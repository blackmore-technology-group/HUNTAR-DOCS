Switching species

HuntAR clears the previous terrain-score context, activates the selected species profile and rescans visible terrain. A terrain relationship for one species should not automatically be carried over to another.

Scientific interpretation

HuntAR distinguishes habitat/use intelligence, survey detectability and animal presence. A habitat score is not direct evidence that an animal is present. Aggregate context is not automatically treated as a detection or nondetection.

Field rule: use the species profile to prioritize where to look and how to reason about terrain—not to replace tracks, sign, glassing, local knowledge, regulations or direct observation.
